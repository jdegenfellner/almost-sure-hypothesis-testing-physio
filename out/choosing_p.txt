=== (3) the implicit cost of a type I relative to a type II error ===
A level alpha minimises w*alpha + beta(alpha) when w = phi(c-ncp)/(2*phi(c)),
with c the critical value and ncp = delta*sqrt(n)/2.  Reading that identity
backwards gives the relative cost each rule commits one to.
